Manufacturing Process

ULTRASONICATION
FOR PRECISE
DISSOLUTION

Ultrasonication sends sound-wave vibrations through a solvent, creating tiny bubbles that rapidly form and collapse. This is known as cavitation.

When those bubbles collapse, they create small zones of intense mixing, shear force, and localized heat that dramatically improve the dissolution and dispersion of compounds into the SNEDDS matrix.

Breaking up powder clumps for more uniform particle size

Increasing contact between compound and solvent

Disrupting particles stuck together

Speeding up dissolution and improving dispersion

Note: Ultrasonication does not make every compound dissolve. A compound still needs to be compatible with the solvent. If a material is insoluble, ultrasonication may help disperse it more evenly, but will not create a true solution.
